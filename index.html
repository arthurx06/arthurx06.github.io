Current Research & Reproduction

China Macro | Python (SF Fed · Bloomberg · EastMoney) | PCA · Fernandez-Denton · SVAR Quarterly Chinese GDP Dataset Construction & Forecasting Replication — Chen, Higgins & Zha (2024), NBER Working Paper
  • Reproduced the full pipeline for constructing quarterly real GDP-exp for China from 2001 to 2025, correcting for the artificial smoothness in NBS official quarterly data — a documented problem in the literature where the NBS back-interpolates quarterly figures from annual totals.
  • Built the alternative China Cyclical Activity Tracker (C-CAT) by extracting the first principal component (PC1) via PCA across 8 monthly hard-data indicators: industrial production, electricity output, exports, fixed asset investment, railway freight, retail sales, floor space started, and consumer confidence. PC1 explains 57.5% of total variance with all factor loadings positive — confirming a strong common economic cycle. Key methodological choice: indicators are not detrended before PCA (unlike the SF Fed version), preserving the full trend-plus-cycle signal needed for interpolation.
  • Applied the Fernandez (1981) constrained interpolation method to distribute known NBS annual real GDP-exp into quarterly observations guided by the C-CAT. The Fernandez slope coefficient β₁ = 1.18 — statistically confirming NBS smoothing, since β₁ = 1.0 would indicate no distortion. Applied Denton (1971) proportional adjustment for exact annual adding-up at Economic Census anchor years.
  • Decomposed quarterly GDP into six expenditure components (household consumption, government consumption, GFCF, inventories, exports, imports) using component-specific monthly interpolators plus a common C-CAT adjustment factor — ensuring all components are consistent with the corrected aggregate rather than NBS's smooth version.
  • Estimated a four-variable Structural VAR (GDP growth, electricity, IP, exports) on the corrected quarterly series with Cholesky identification (ordering: physical indicators → aggregate output) to produce forward-looking impulse responses and GDP growth nowcasts.

load SF Fed YoY growth rates # for 8 indicators; load Bloomberg annual NBS national accounts and deflators; # load SAFE BOP quarterly exports/imports (2013Q1+). # SECTION II — C-CAT CONSTRUCTION (PCA) # Convert SF Fed YoY% → quarterly log-differences (YoY / 400). # Standardise each series to zero mean, unit variance (required: PCA is # scale-sensitive — without this, noisiest series dominates). # Extract PC1 via sklearn PCA: explains 57.5% of variance across all 8. # All factor loadings positive — confirms strong common economic cycle. # Key choice: no detrending before PCA (unlike SF Fed version), preserving # trend signal needed for the interpolation step. # SECTION III — FERNANDEZ-DENTON INTERPOLATION # OLS on anchor years: annual_GDP_growth = β₀ + β₁ × annual_avg(C-CAT). # β₁ = 1.176 — statistically proves NBS smoothing (if accurate: β₁ = 1.0). # Apply quarterly: growth_q = β₀ + β₁ × C-CAT_q, then shift all 4 quarters # uniformly so their average matches NBS annual growth at anchor years. # Denton proportional adjustment: scale factor ≈ 1.0 enforces exact adding-up. # Two stages: loose constraint 2001–2018 (anchor years only); # tight constraint 2019–2025 (every year — NBS improved post-2018). # SECTION IV — EXPENDITURE SUBCOMPONENTS # Each of 6 components (HH consumption, Govt, GFCF, inventories, exports, # imports) uses a component-specific monthly interpolator plus the C-CAT # adjustment factor (= our GDP growth − NBS GDP growth), ensuring all # components are consistent with the corrected aggregate, not NBS's smooth version. # Final consistency: proportional scaling of domestic components + quadratic # solve for exports/imports balance: X·a − M/a = NX_target. # SECTION V — SVAR FORECASTING (Stata) # 4-variable SVAR: GDP growth, electricity, IP, exports on corrected series. # Cholesky identification: electricity → IP → exports → GDP (physical # indicators causally prior within quarter; GDP aggregate last). # ADF stationarity tests → lag selection (varsoc) → Johansen cointegration # check → svar estimation → irf/fevd → rolling out-of-sample forecast. # C-CAT at −1.7 to −2.4 std dev in late 2025 signals below-trend activity; # SVAR impulse responses point to downside risk to NBS 5% target in 2026Q1.
